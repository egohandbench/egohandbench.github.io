EgoHandBench: A Unified Benchmark for Egocentric 3D Hand Reconstruction

We present EgoHandBench, a unified benchmark for egocentric 3D hand reconstruction. It standardizes six public datasets, filters out frames with unreliable annotations, and establishes fixed training and test splits. We release the dataset along with the official evaluator and ready-to-run baselines.

Description

EgoHandBench evaluates 3D hand reconstruction from egocentric video. Given an RGB video and camera intrinsics, each method predicts the 3D joint positions of both hands in camera coordinates. The task is challenging due to object occlusion, rapid hand motion, hands leaving the view, and head-driven camera motion.

Egocentric human video is a scalable source of manipulation data. Recent robot policies[1, 2, 3, 4] leverage hand trajectories extracted from such videos for training. However, existing egocentric hand datasets differ in annotation formats, data splits, and evaluation metrics, making cross-method comparisons difficult.

Data Curation

EgoHandBench unifies six public egocentric datasets: ARCTIC, H2O, HOT3D, OakInk2, EgoDemo (Pro and Standard), and Gen-HumanEgo. We convert all annotations to MANO in camera coordinates, mirror the right-hand model for left hands, and fit MANO where only 3D joints are given. Every clip uses a pinhole camera with per-frame intrinsics.

We remove frames and clips with unreliable (missing or inaccurate) annotations, identified by automatic checks and manual review, as well as frames with unusable images. The six in-domain subsets (ARCTIC, H2O, HOT3D, OakInk2, EgoDemo Pro, and EgoDemo Standard) have fixed training and test splits. Gen-HumanEgo is test-only and measures in-the-wild generalization.
