Evaluation Protocol

Scoring

The leaderboard ranks methods by their overall scores, computed from per-dataset metric scores. Each metric is normalized within each dataset: the ideal value receives 100 points, the worst evaluated result receives 0, and values in between are interpolated linearly:

score = 100 × (v − v_worst) / (v_ideal − v_worst)

Here, v is the metric value, v_ideal is 100% for FAcc and F1 and 0 for the other metrics, and v_worst is the worst value among all evaluated results on that dataset, including both official and EgoHandBench-trained checkpoints.

The dataset score is the mean of the metric scores on that dataset. Dataset scores are averaged separately over the in-domain group (six subsets with fixed training and test splits) and the in-the-wild group (Gen-HumanEgo, test-only), and the two groups are weighted equally in the overall score:

Overall = 0.5 × mean(in-domain dataset scores) + 0.5 × mean(in-the-wild dataset scores)

The "All Datasets" table reports raw metric values averaged with the same group weighting. Each segment of a leaderboard bar shows the contribution of one metric to the overall score.

Metric Definitions

In each frame, a method predicts at most one left and one right hand, each represented by 21 3D joints in camera coordinates. MANO predictions are converted to the same joints. Let p_j and g_j denote the predicted and ground-truth (GT) positions of joint j in millimeters, w the wrist joint, π_K the perspective projection with camera intrinsics K, V the set of visible GT joints, and W and H the image width and height in pixels.

The suffix -p denotes a penalty for missed hands. These metrics are computed over all visible GT hands: each true positive is evaluated with its prediction, and each unmatched hand receives a penalty error. Every metric is computed over all test clips of a dataset, and implementation details are given in the official evaluation code.

FAcc = correct frames / all frames

Hand detection F1. The harmonic mean of detection precision and recall over all test hands.

F1 = 2TP / (2TP + FP + FN)

Absolute joint error. The mean 3D joint error in camera coordinates, without alignment.

mean_j ||p_j − g_j||₂

MPJPE-p ↓

Wrist-aligned joint error. The mean 3D joint error after aligning the wrists.

mean_j ||(p_j − p_w) − (g_j − g_w)||₂

PA-p ↓
mm

Procrustes-aligned joint error. The mean 3D joint error after aligning scale s, rotation R, and translation t.

mean_j ||s R p_j + t − g_j||₂

EPE2D-p ↓
px

2D joint error. The mean pixel distance between projected predicted and ground-truth joints, over visible ground-truth joints, with each distance capped at the image diagonal.

mean_{j∈V} min(||π_K(p_j) − π_K(g_j)||₂, √(W² + H²))

Jitter ↓
mm/frame²

Temporal jitter. The mean acceleration of the predicted joints over consecutive true-positive frames.

mean_{j,t} ||p_j(t+1) − 2p_j(t) + p_j(t−1)||₂

Detection and Matching

A hand is considered visible if at least one of its joints lies in front of the camera and projects inside the image. Only visible hands are evaluated. In each frame, predictions are matched to GT hands based on the intersection over union (IoU) of the 2D bounding boxes of their projected joints, with a preference for the GT hand of the same handedness. A visible GT hand is a true positive (TP) if it is matched to a prediction of the same handedness with nonzero IoU, and a false negative (FN) otherwise. We refer to FNs as unmatched hands. A visible prediction is a false positive (FP) if its matched GT hand has zero IoU with it or the opposite handedness.

Unmatched Hands

Unmatched hands are retained in the -p metrics, each contributing a penalty error. For MPJPE-abs-p, MPJPE-p, and EPE2D-p, the penalty is the error of a default prediction: the MANO mean hand of the same handedness, with its wrist placed at a depth of 0.5 m on the camera ray through the image center.

Since PA-p aligns each prediction to the GT before measuring the error, the default prediction could obtain a lower error than an actual prediction. PA-p therefore uses a separate penalty: the root-mean-square distance of the GT joints from their centroid, which is an upper bound on the Procrustes-aligned error of any non-degenerate prediction.

Inference Speed

All methods are timed on a single NVIDIA A100 80 GB GPU using the same 400-frame EgoDemo Standard clip (608 × 480), with two hands processed per frame. For timing only, missed detections are supplemented with boxes derived from the GT 2D joints. The reported time excludes video decoding, file I/O, model loading, warm-up, and SLAM-based camera estimation.

Offline FPS is measured at the fastest tested batch size, and stream FPS by feeding one frame at a time. Both are reported as the median of three runs. Peak GPU memory is measured during offline inference. Speed and memory are reported for reference and do not affect the score.
